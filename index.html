My research interest mainly falls in system reliability, in particular, GPUs (Graphics Processing Units), HPC (High-Performance Computing), and autonomous driving. I received a Ph.D. degree in the Department of Computer Science at William & Mary in 2022, under the supervision of Prof. Evgenia Smirni. Before coming to W&M, I got my bachelor's degree in computer science from University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in 2016. Check my CV here

I am at capacity for hiring new students.

However, for any student from anywhere, if you want some general advices or if you have some confusions, for example, research, career path, rejected papers, not getting along with your advisor, etc, you are welcome to send an email to me and I will find some time for a quick chat. Sometimes an outsider helps; I got many help from various (random) outsiders during my PhD study.

If you do not satisfy the prerequisite but you are still interested in taking the class, feel free to send me an email to see whether I can waive the prerequisite. Just my two cents: There are people who can enjoy doing research, enjoy(?) being tortured by paper rejections, and feel happy dealing with students, but not everyone. Given the unsatisfying PhD/faculty salary, if you could not enjoy the job itself, why don't you find an industry job that has less pressure and is paid higher?
